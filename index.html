// Vision & Mission

I am on a mission to build revolutionary, foundational technology that is inherently safe, resilient, and sovereign for all users.

Most modern computing is fragile, bloated, and unsafe by design. True safety cannot be an afterthought patched on top of broken layers—it must be architected into the bedrock: from kernel primitives and memory safety to decentralized, zero-trust infrastructure.

// About Me

I am a Linux Systems Administrator and POSIX systems engineer with 3+ years of intensive experience in low-level Linux internals, bare-metal hypervisors (QEMU/KVM), and hybrid cloud environments (Azure & AWS).

I build systems software from the bottom up—focusing on lockless concurrency, kernel interfaces (/proc, cgroups v2, namespaces), defense-in-depth networking, and high-performance tooling in C, Rust, and Go.
